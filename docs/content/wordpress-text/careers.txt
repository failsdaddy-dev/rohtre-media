[H2] FAQ'S
[H2] What do you want to know about the hiring process at ROHTREMEDIA?
What is the main selection criteria?
We focus on your ability to complete the assigned task within the given deadline. Speed, creativity, and attention to detail matter most.
Do I need prior experience to apply?
Not always. We value skills and dedication over just experience. If you can prove yourself through the task, you stand a good chance.
How does the hiring process work?
Step 1: Apply.
Step 2: Shortlisted candidates receive a task.
Step 3: Submit the task within the deadline.
Step 4: If selected, we’ll schedule an interview/discussion.
What type of tasks can I expect?
Tasks depend on the role. For creative roles, it could be editing, design, or content samples. For HR/BD roles, it may involve outreach, research, or strategy assignments.
How quickly will I know the results?
Usually within 2-3 working days after task submission. We try to be quick, but timelines may vary depending on the role.
Do you provide remote opportunities?
Yes. Many of our roles and internships are remote, unless specifically mentioned otherwise.
How important is meeting deadlines?
Very important. At Rohtre Media, deadlines define reliability. Missing them without reason can reduce your chances of selection.
Do you consider freshers?
Yes. If you’re skilled, eager to learn, and can prove yourself with the task, we welcome you.
What qualities do you look for in candidates?
Accountability, adaptability, speed, creativity, and willingness to take ownership.
Will I get a certificate/experience letter?
Yes. Interns and employees completing their tenure or project successfully receive official recognition.
